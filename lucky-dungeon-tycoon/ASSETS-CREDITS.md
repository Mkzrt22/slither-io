## City building models (`web/models/city/`)

**[Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza)**. Models licensed **CC-BY 3.0** require
attribution (given below); **CC0** models are public domain and credited as a
courtesy. Embedded textures were downscaled (to 512² / 1024²) for a smaller,
mobile-friendly bundle; geometry is unchanged.
